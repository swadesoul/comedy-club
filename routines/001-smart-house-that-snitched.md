## Performance and production cue draft

**0:00–0:05, station liner:** "You're live at Maestro Comedy Club! Let the laughter begin!" Brief branded sting and applause, then bring room tone down.

**0:05–0:35, setup. MARCUS:**
"I bought a smart house last month. Everything's connected. Refrigerator, thermostat, doorbell, lights. Even the toilet got Wi-Fi!"
[Small chuckle, 1.1s.]
"First night, my wife said, 'Baby, this house is gonna make our lives so much easier.' I said, 'Wonderful. But somebody needs to tell this house one thing...'"
[Half-second silent hold.]
"'What happens in this house STAYS in this house!'"
[Strong laugh, target 2.5s.]

**0:35–1:12, escalation. MARCUS:**
"Three days later, my refrigerator sent my wife a notification. Said, 'Marcus consumed cheesecake at 2:47 a.m.'"
[Laugh, target 2s.]
"My wife comes downstairs: 'Who been eating cheesecake?' I said, 'I don't know! Maybe somebody broke in!' She said, 'Then why does the security camera show you eating it with a FORK and a flashlight?'"
[Big laugh, target 3s.]
"I'm paying a mortgage on a house that's building a case against me!"
[Secondary laugh, 1.8s.]

**1:12–1:48, crowd work. MARCUS:**
"Anybody here got one of these smart houses?"
[Scattered cheers.]
AUDIENCE MAN, front-right: "Mine listens to everything!"
MARCUS: "EVERYTHING? Brother, you living in a house or testifying before Congress?"
[Big laugh, 2.5s.]
AUDIENCE WOMAN, left-center: "Just unplug it!"
MARCUS: "Ma'am, I tried that! The house sent me an email saying, 'We noticed you're trying to disconnect from the relationship.'"
[Laugh travels left to center, 3s.]
"Even the HOUSE won't let me break up peacefully!"
[Secondary chuckle, 1.5s.]

**1:48–2:25, twist. MARCUS:**
"Then yesterday, I got into an argument with my wife. I said, 'I never said that!' You know what happened? The living room speaker turned on by itself!"
[Anticipation chuckle.]
"It said, 'Actually, Marcus, you said that Tuesday at 8:14 p.m. Would you like me to play the recording?'"
[Large laugh, 3.5s.]
"I said, 'Whose side are you ON?!' That speaker said, 'Based on household usage patterns... hers.'"
[Big laugh with scattered applause, 3s.]

**2:25–3:00, callback. MARCUS:**
"So last night, I finally figured out how to fix it. I went into the settings. Changed every password. Turned off every notification. I told my wife, 'Baby, we're taking our privacy back!' She said, 'Good!' Then, in the dark, I heard a little voice from the kitchen..."
[Full silence, target 1s.]
REFRIGERATOR, original synthetic character voice: "Marcus... you still owe us for the cheesecake."
[Large laugh.]
MARCUS: "I TOLD YOU! WHAT HAPPENS IN THIS HOUSE—"
AUDIENCE, several separate voices: "STAYS IN THIS HOUSE!"
MARCUS: "EXCEPT MY BUSINESS! THE HOUSE GOT A PODCAST!"
[Biggest laugh, applause, brief sting. Fade by 3:00.]
